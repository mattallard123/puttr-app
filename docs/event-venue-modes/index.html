Event Mode and Venue Mode

Legacy Party mode splits into two modes for Puttr Club members. Event Mode is for a one-off corporate day; Venue Mode is for a screen that runs every night in a bar or simulator venue. Both are set up from Settings, branded from the app, and saved to the host's profile as before. In the 3D worlds the brand goes onto in-world surfaces instead of background photos.

Nothing here is built. Brands in the mockups are invented; switch kits to see every screen re-skin.

Entry and set-up

From Settings, saved to your profile

A new Host section in Settings has one row, Event & Venue mode, shown to every adult account. Non-members get the Club page. Members pick a mode, then land in the brand kit editor. The kit saves to the profile through the same /party/brandkit route legacy uses, so any device signed in to that account picks it up. Start turns the device over to the mode; hold the logo and enter the staff PIN to get back.

From legacy

Kept

Brand and co-host logos, primary and secondary colours, title, one line under it, leaderboard title, QR link and message, game list with order and a featured game, clear leaderboard.

Dropped

Background photos

Attract, menu and in-game backgrounds and their dim levels. The colours light the 3D stage instead. Existing legacy kits carry over without them.

New

Partner logos

Up to five, rotating on one LED panel. The last panel is always Puttr.

New

Staff PIN

Four digits, replacing the five-tap exit. Unlocks: exit, remove a score, new event, volume.

The brand in the world

Eight places a kit shows up

Most scenes already have places where a sponsor would be in real life, and today they show the Puttr logo. The engine paints the kit onto them in every game. Scenes without boards (Balloon Pop, Turf War) use the video board and tee mark only.

Shared by both modes

The foundation

From legacy

Typed names

The Player picker in host mode shows typed names only (no profiles on the host's account), 1 to 4 seats. Guest putts save under the host's account, as in legacy.

Changed

Private leaderboard

On the in-world video board, the attract screen and the web signage page. Keyed to a fixed id for the host instead of the display name, so a rename never breaks the link.

Changed

Custom games

The host's Standard and Center Cut custom games appear in the game list alongside catalogue games, each switchable and orderable like any other.

New

Attract loop

After 90 seconds idle the screen returns to a slow camera move around the branded stage with the board. A putt wakes it.

One-off corporate day

Event Mode

For an operator like Dryvebox or Wego Golf running a client day. Someone is at the screen, guests have never used Puttr, and the client wants a winner at the end.

New

One board per event

Start new event (staff PIN) archives the current board and starts an empty one. The kit stays.

From legacy

Featured challenge

The featured game leads the attract screen. Usually a custom game, like the Zero Torque Challenge.

From legacy

QR to the board

The attract and final screens show a QR to the event's web leaderboard so guests can follow along on their phones.

Every night, unattended

Venue Mode

For a simulator bar or entertainment venue with one or more bays. Regulars come back, so members play as themselves and the house records are worth chasing.

New

Scan to play as you

The seat screen shows a QR. A member scans it in the Puttr app and takes a seat with their name, picture and level. Their games and putts save to their own profile. Others type a name.

New

Sign-out

Members are signed out when the group taps End session, or after five minutes idle.

New

Rolling boards

Tonight, This week and All time per game. Member scores carry a verified mark.

New

Several bays

Every device signed in to the host's account shares the kit and the board. Each one can be named (Bay 1, Bay 2) in its own settings.

Build order

  1. Brand layer in the 3D engine. One source for logo, partner logos and palette in place of the Puttr logo paths repeated across about twelve scene files and the colour constants in Make.cs.
  2. Settings entry and kit editor. Club gate, mode choice, the editor with live preview, reading and writing the existing /party/brandkit kit.
  3. Host shell. Attract loop, typed-name picker, curated game list with custom games, staff PIN, leaderboard on a fixed host id.
  4. Event Mode. Event board, New event, web board QR.
  5. Venue Mode. Multi-seat QR sign-in (built on the TV sign-in), member saves, rolling boards, bay names.
