Puttr 2 · XP and levels · round two

Every place XP shows up

Before any XP is built into the app: each screen where a Player will earn it, see it or spend it, drawn in the Broadcast system. The first XP page (22 November) predates Broadcast, so its amber panels, coloured ranks and rounded tiles are replaced here.

Nothing on this page is built. The rules and the award values are the XP plan's; anything I added is marked.

Round three, 25 November: the reward is status in the community (Matt). Each rank now has its own colour, shown wherever a Player's name appears, and there is one Levels sheet that explains the ladder. The unlockable looks are gone.

The rules, unchanged

Level is effort. PQS is skill.

  • 1,000 XP a level, flat. Nine ranks, each reached at a total: Rookie, Dance Floor, Weekender, Scratch, Club Champ, State Am, Tour, Major, Legend.
  • The reward is status. A rank is a colour everyone sees beside your name. A level never makes a putt easier or a score higher.
  • The API awards XP when a game saves, so every device agrees.
  • PQS stays the big number. Level sits under it, never over it.
  • One place explains each award: the final screen lists what the game just paid.
The ladder

Nine ranks, nine colours

A Player's rank is a small badge in the rank's colour with their level on it. It works like the tiers of a loyalty card: anyone can tell a Tour from a Rookie at a glance. Each rank is reached at a total, and the bands widen so that Legend is very hard (Matt, 25 November).

Where you earn it

The XP panel

One component, used wherever XP is paid. It sits under PQS and above Play again on the game over page, so the score and PQS still lead.

  • Head: "XP earned" and the total.
  • Rows: one for each award this game paid, and only those.
  • Bar: 4 px. Brand green is what the Player had, white is what was just added.
  • Foot: level and rank on the left, XP to the next level on the right.

Who gets one

The signed-in Player, and each Profile on the account for their own games. A guest, Puttr as the opponent, test putts and host mode get no panel at all, not an empty one.

Local games with two to four Players

Only signed-in Profiles earn. The panel shows the XP of the Player whose phone it is; the standings above it stay as they are.

When the bar fills

Level up

One page, shown between game over and whatever comes next. It is a pushed page like any other, not a modal over the game.

  • It shows the badge, since that is what other Players will see.
  • One main button, Continue, and one secondary to the Levels sheet.
  • Two levels in one game (possible with a one-time award) show the higher number.

What changed from the first drawing

The first drawing gave every level an unlock (a console light, a ball trail). Those are gone: the thing a Player earns is the rank and its colour. An ordinary level-up changes the number on the badge; every tenth level changes the colour, and that is the moment the page is built around.

Where you see it

Profile, the Levels sheet and Home

Should Profile have a ? for XP

Yes. It is the same pattern Improve already uses for Putting Quality Score: a ? as the link on the section header. Here it opens the Levels sheet, the one page that explains the whole scheme: where the Player stands, the six ranks and their colours, then every award and its value.

  • The values are read from the API, the same table that pays them, so the page can never disagree with what a game awards.
  • The XP panel and Home's level row open the same sheet, so a Player who wonders about a row is one tap away.
  • It is the only explanation of XP. There is no first-run tour or first-look strip, so this sheet is where XP is explained.

Home

The XP plan put a level chip in Home's header. That header already holds its two actions (the Puttr status pill and the bell), and the guidelines allow two. So the level is one list row under today's session, which opens the Levels sheet.

Profile

The avatar carries the rank ring and the name carries the badge, exactly as other Players see them. The level block sits under the name, above the all-time strip. The six attributes from the sports UI spec are still undecided and are left out.

Other Players

Where your level is seen

Everywhere a Player's name appears, the badge is beside it; wherever their avatar is big enough, the ring is round it. That is the whole reward, so it has to be consistent: one badge, one ring, the same six colours, in every list.

  • Boards still sort by score. The badge never changes an order or a pairing.
  • Kid profiles have a rank like anyone else.

Rank-ups in the feed

The community feed on Home gets one new kind of post: a Player reaching a new rank. Five in a lifetime, so it never floods. Ordinary level-ups are not posted. Every other post carries the badge beside the name.

On purpose

Where XP does not show

During a gameNo XP ticks up while putting. The HUD is score, tee and the putt read. XP is counted once, on the final page.
Sorting and pairingBoards rank scores and Online pairs on skill. The badge is shown there but never used to order or match Players.
ImproveThe tab is about the stroke and PQS. Sessions filmed there pay XP on their own final page, not on the tab.
Push notificationsLevel-ups happen with the app open, on the final page. Nothing is pushed.
Host mode and venuesGuests at an event earn nothing and see no panel.
The Coach's deskA Coach looking at a Player sees PQS and the stroke. Level is not coaching information.
To decide

Before it is built

  • The colour of new XP. Drawn white on the bar, with the "XP earned" label in bright green. The bar stays brand green at every rank, so rank colours stay on the badge. The first drawing used amber, which Broadcast keeps for time running out and streaks.
  • Today's session. The plan pays "daily putts" (25 each for five, 50 for all). The app has today's session instead. I mapped it to 125 for the session and 50 for finishing, 175 a day. Is that right?
  • Home. A level row under the session card, or change the guidelines to allow a third item in the header?
  • Backfill. History puts long-time Players in Tour on day one. Keep that, or start everyone at 1?
  • A daily cap. Without one, 3 ft makes pay 5 XP each without limit.
  • The six rank colours. Slate, green, blue, purple, orange, gold. Orange sits close to the amber used for streaks; white (platinum) is the alternative for Major. Once agreed they go into the guidelines as rank colours, used only on the badge, the ring and the rank-up title.
  • One colour a rank, not a level. I read "each level has a colour" as each of the six ranks, with the level number on the badge. Fifty distinct colours would not be recognisable.
  • Games without makes. Tracks and the arcade games have no makes or center cuts. Do they pay the 50 for finishing only, or get rows of their own?
  • The Front Nine. Left out of this page. If it comes back, its hole awards are more rows in the same panel.
