The rules, from the spec

Level is effort. PQS is skill.

  • 1,000 XP a level, flat. Level 15 costs the same as level 2, so the bar always moves at the same speed.
  • Six ranks, one every ten levels: Rookie, Fairway, Scratch, Tour, Major, Legend.
  • Unlocks are looks only. A level never makes a putt easier or a score higher.
  • The API awards XP when a game saves, so every device agrees, and it is backfilled from a Player's history.
  • PQS stays the big number. Level and XP sit under it, never over it.
  • One place explains each award: the final screen lists what the game just paid.

How fast that is
  • A typical game pays about 140, so a level is about seven games.
  • Three games a week is a level about every two and a half weeks.
  • A game and the daily putts every day is about two levels a week.
  • Legend (level 50) is 49,000 XP, about 350 games at 140 each.
Backfill from history
  • The spec says XP is backfilled. It does not say how.
  • One way: 50 for every finished game and 5 for every make on record. Wins and center cuts are left out, since old rows may not hold them.
  • Example: 400 games and 3,200 makes is 36,000 XP, which starts that Player at level 37, Tour.
Before anything is built

Decisions I need from you

  1. Are the numbers right? The spec calls them a first proposal to tune. The table above is that proposal plus my additions.
  2. How should backfill work? Long-time Players would start at a high level under the example above. That may be what you want, or you may want everyone to start at 1.
  3. Is there a daily cap? Without one, grinding 3 ft makes pays 5 XP each without limit.
  4. Who earns it? My assumption: the signed-in Player and Profiles on the account earn their own; guests and Puttr earn none; test putts earn none.
  5. What are the unlocks? The spec names one. A list of looks is needed for at least the first 20 levels.
  6. Do the six attributes stay? They are still open in the spec and are drawn on the card here.
